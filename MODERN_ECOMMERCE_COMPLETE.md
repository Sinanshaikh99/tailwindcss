## 🚀 How to Add Products Now:

1. Login as admin
2. Go to Product Management
3. Click "Add New Product"
4. Fill in:
   - Upload 4 images
   - Name
   - Category
   - Price
   - Stock
   - Description (brief)
   - Details (specs)
   - Material (fabric)
   - Care (instructions)
   - Sizes, colors
5. Save - Done!
